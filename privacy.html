Privacy policy

The short version: we collect nothing.

SupportPrivacy policy

What Solo stores

Your trades, journal entries, and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local database. Solo has no servers. Nothing you enter is transmitted anywhere.

What Solo collects

Nothing. Solo contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs, no third-party code that phones home, and it makes no network requests. There are no accounts and no sign-up.

On-device intelligence

When you use natural-language trade entry on a supported device, the text is parsed by Apple Intelligence on the device itself. Your text is not sent to us or to any third party by Solo.

Imports

CSV files you import are read locally and stored in the app's local database. They are never uploaded.

Deleting your data

Delete a trade in the app to remove it and its journal. Deleting the app removes the entire ledger from your device.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above — for example, optional iCloud sync — this policy will be updated first, and any such feature will be opt-in.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the support tracker.
