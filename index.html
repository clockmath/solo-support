Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Solo has no servers, no account, and no cloud component. Deleting the app deletes your ledger — export or back up your device first.

How does the natural-language entry work?
On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the parsing runs on-device — your text never leaves your phone. On other devices, a built-in parser handles common phrasings.

Which broker exports can I import?
Wealthsimple activity exports (activities-export*.csv). Re-importing is safe: duplicates are skipped and manual entries are never touched.

Is Solo financial advice?
No. Solo is a journal. It records what you did and helps you reflect on it. It does not recommend, predict, or execute trades.
